## AI Chat Widget for SaaS

Add an always-on AI support agent to your website with one script tag. The BoostGPT chat widget gives your users instant help 24/7, trained on your product knowledge.

## What You Can Do

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Deploy Chat Widget" icon="message-square">
    Add a floating chat bubble to any website — always available for users
  </Card>

  <Card title="Customize Branding" icon="palette">
    Match the widget to your product — colors, icon, greetings, and open style
  </Card>

  <Card title="Identify Users" icon="lock">
    Sign a JWT to auto-identify logged-in users via SSO
  </Card>

  <Card title="Track Analytics" icon="chart-line">
    Monitor widget engagement and conversations
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## How It Works

**Always-on support:**
```
User visits your site → Widget bubble appears → User clicks → Instant AI chat
```

The agent answers from your product knowledge, can call tools, and keeps conversation history across sessions when SSO is configured.

## Use Cases

- **Customer Support** - Always-on AI agent answering product questions 24/7
- **Product Questions** - Answer plan, feature, and pricing questions instantly
- **Onboarding Help** - Guide users through signup and setup
- **Lead Capture** - Collect contact details from interested visitors
